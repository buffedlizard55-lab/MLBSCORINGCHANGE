Every official MLB scoring change, every error of the season with the model's chance (0–100) that the official scorer changes it to a hit, and each play's final result. Every row links to official sources so you can verify it yourself. Plays changed from a hit to an error (usually a single) have their own 📉 Hit → Error section here and on the Replay Feed — deliberately out of the primary alerts. 📊 Stat Changes lists the changes to a batter's runs, hits or RBI (the only ones in the main alerts), 🧮 Pitching Stats the changes to pitching lines, and 📅 Error Log every error of every game with its video. For live games, use the Replay Feed (🎯 Error Watch, ✏️ Scoring Changes, 🧮 Pitching Stats, 📉 Hit → Error and ⚖️ Scoring Pending tabs).
